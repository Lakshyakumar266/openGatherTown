SoppyCraft – Forest Houses Top Down Pixel Pack
======================================

A top-down pixel art environment pack featuring forest houses, paths, vegetation and water props.
Designed for RPGs, farming sims and narrative games.


CONTENTS
--------
53 sprites across the following categories:

    Houses          4 sprites
    Trees           2 sprites
    Bushes          2 sprites
    Grass           7 sprites + 1 background strip
    Flowers         5 sprites
    Flower pots     2 sprites
    Clay pots       3 sprites
    Mud paths       4 sprites + 1 end piece
    Stone paths     9 sprites
    Water troughs   4 sprites (empty/full variants)
    Water pumps     5 sprites (empty/full variants)


FOLDER STRUCTURE
----------------
Pack_forest_houses/
|-- Forest_Houses.png                  <- preview scene
|-- forest_houses_spritesheet.png      <- full spritesheet (16x16 grid)
`-- Sprites/
    |-- pack_house_001.png
    |-- pack_house_002.png
    |-- pack_tree_001.png
    `-- ... (individual sprites, free size)


FORMAT NOTES
------------
- Spritesheet: 16x16 grid, PNG
- Individual sprites: variable sizes, PNG
- No JSON metadata included
- No Aseprite source files included

Note: preview scenes use props from the Indoor Plants Pixel Pack, sold separately
at soppycraft.itch.io
